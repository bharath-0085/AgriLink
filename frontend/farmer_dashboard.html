Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about Agri Link.

Navigate to the Crop Recommendation tab from the sidebar. Enter your soil metrics (Nitrogen, Phosphorus, Potassium, Soil pH) along with rainfall and temperature figures. Click "Get Recommendation" to receive machine-learning predicted crops with expected yield and water requirements.
In the Disease Detection module, upload or capture a clear, well-lit photo of an infected leaf. Our deep-learning computer vision model scans for pathology symptoms (like early blight, rust, or powdery mildew) and provides immediate diagnosis, organic bio-treatments, and chemical controls.
Visit the Labour Hiring tab to browse verified agricultural workers by location, specialization, and daily wage. For machinery, open Equipment Rental to reserve tractors, harvesters, or power tillers with flexible per-hour or per-day booking terms. Track all schedules under My Bookings.
Go to the Marketplace tab and click "List New Crop". Specify your crop variety, quantity available (in quintals/kg), expected price per unit, and harvest date. Verified buyers will contact you directly or submit purchase orders that you can accept or decline.
You can check eligibility criteria for PM-KISAN, PM Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY), and state fertilizer subsidies through the Government Scheme Portal resource below, or ask our AI Assistant for personalized scheme qualification guidelines based on your landholding size.
Open the Settings tab from the sidebar. Under Profile Settings, you can update your Full Name, Phone, Village, District, State, Pincode, Preferred Language, Farm Size, and Primary Crops. Click "Save Changes" to save your updated information across the dashboard.
Yes. All communication on Agri Link is secured using SSL/TLS 256-bit encryption. Financial transactions and payments use bank-grade gateway partners, and sensitive personal information is strictly protected and never shared with unverified third parties.
You can call our toll-free Kisan Call Centre (1800-180-1551) directly during operating hours (Mon–Sat, 8 AM – 8 PM) to speak with a government-certified agricultural expert, or submit a support ticket selecting "Crop Advice" with your field photos attached.
