WHO WE ARE & OUR MISSION

Empowering Every Hand
That Feeds The Nation

Agri Link is India’s next-generation agricultural bridge connecting farmers, field labourers, equipment owners, and crop buyers into one unified, transparent digital ecosystem.

For generations, Indian farmers have faced volatile commodity pricing, unexpected crop disease outbreaks, seasonal labour shortages, and expensive machinery. Agri Link eliminates these barriers through AI-powered disease diagnosis and crop recommendations, direct crop trading, on-demand machinery rentals, and direct labour hiring.

STEP-BY-STEP PROCESS

How Agri Link Works

Get started in minutes. Follow our 6 simple steps to access diagnosis, farm workers, machinery, and fair produce markets.

1

Register or Login

Enter your mobile phone number, select your role as Farmer, Labourer, or Buyer, and complete one-click OTP verification.

2

Select Required Service

Choose from Crop Disease Scanner, Labour Hiring, Machinery Rental, Produce Marketplace, or AI Crop Recommendation.

3

Enter or Upload Information

Upload an infected leaf photo, enter soil NPK levels, list your harvest with target quantity, or specify needed machinery.

4

AI & Service Processing

Our convolutional neural networks and smart matching algorithms process your data in seconds to find the best solutions.

5

View Results or Options

Review diagnosis confidence and suggested cures, browse nearby available tractors, or see matching labour profiles.

6

Complete Required Action

Confirm rental dates, finalize buyer contracts, hire workers for field tasks, or apply recommended treatments.
